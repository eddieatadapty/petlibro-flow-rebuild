# Petlibro Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/petlibro-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Petlibro users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Petlibro's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask about the pet, then use the answers everywhere | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | A first meal for their pet before any price | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | One plan for their device, not three lines | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Proof, the yearly saving and the refund at the button | +10–15% ARPU, from a bigger yearly share |
| 05 | Catch the people who close the paywall | +10–15% ARPU, from people who said no to yearly |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Petlibro's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Petlibro. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
